Desserts

Gulab Jamun

Soft, round balls made from khoya (reduced milk) and flour, deep-fried and soaked in a sugar syrup flavored with rose water or cardamo

Rasgulla

Spongy, round sweets made from chenna (cottage cheese) cooked in a sugar syrup, known for their delicate and light texture

Rasmalai

Soft, flattened cheese patties soaked in a creamy, sweetened milk mixture flavored with cardamom, saffron, and garnished with nuts

Halwa

A sweet, dense dessert made from ingredients like semolina (suji), carrots (gajar), or lentils, cooked with ghee, sugar, and nuts. A popular Indian cola known for its strong, spicy flavor and distinctive taste.
