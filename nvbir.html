NV Biryani

Chicken Biryani

A spiced rice dish made with marinated chicken, cooked with basmati rice, fried onions, saffron, and a blend of aromatic spices. Often served with raita

Mutton Biryani

A rich and flavorful biryani made with tender pieces of mutton, basmati rice, and a blend of spices, cooked in layers with fried onions and saffron

Prawn Biryani

A fragrant and spicy rice dish made with prawns, basmati rice, and aromatic spices, layered and cooked together to blend the flavors

Egg Biryani

A biryani made with hard-boiled eggs and basmati rice, cooked with spices, and often garnished with fried onions and fresh herbs
